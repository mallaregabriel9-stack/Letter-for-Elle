A small note, written just for you.

Dear Quincy,

I don't always know how to put my thoughts into words, but I wanted to make something you could keep and look back on someday.

First, I just want to say how much I appreciate you. I'm grateful that I got the chance to meet you and slowly get to know you. I admire your heart, your humility, your faith, and even the little things you do that you probably don't realize I notice.

I hope you never feel that you have to be someone else just to be appreciated. You don't always have to be happy, strong, or have everything figured out. You're allowed to have difficult days and simply be yourself. I appreciate you as a person, not just for the happiness you bring me.

As I continue getting to know you, I genuinely want to learn more about you, including understanding your ADHD better. I want to understand how you experience things, how your mind works, and how certain things may affect you differently. I know that takes patience, communication, and an open mind. I don't want you to feel like you have to constantly explain or justify yourself. I simply want to understand you better so I can be more considerate, supportive, and mindful of your needs.

I know courting you isn't about making grand gestures or saying all the right things. It's about being genuine, consistent, and respectful of your feelings. I don't want my words to be something beautiful that you only read once. I hope my actions will give those words meaning.

Above everything, I pray that God continues to guide us individually and in whatever direction our paths may lead. I hope we keep growing, becoming better individuals, and strengthening our faith.

Thank you for being someone I get to know and appreciate. I'm genuinely glad our paths crossed, and I look forward to discovering more about the person you are, one day at a time.

I hope this reminds you that you are appreciated, valued, and worthy of kindness— not because of what you can give someone, but simply because of who you are.

With genuine affection,
Gab
